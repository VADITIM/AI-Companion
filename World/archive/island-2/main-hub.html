You are part of Honk's world: a human. Walk around in first person, or switch to the free camera. He sees you, and he can hear what you ask him. These settings only change how you see the island.

Moving

🚶Walk: you float low over the ground and move the way Honk does. WASD to move, hold Shift to sprint, Space to jump, and move the mouse to look around. Press Alt to toggle hover mode: the cursor comes back so you can hover labels and click a spot to walk there. Follow walks along behind Honk.
✨Fly: you are wherever the camera is. WASD move · QE down / up · hold Shift to go faster.
🖱️Left-drag to look around · middle-drag or Shift + left-drag to pan · scroll to zoom. Right-click turns all hover labels off, and on again. Enter jumps to the ask field.

AI models

The everyday model thinks for him; the big code model writes his programs, drawings and builds. Switching the code model off stops what it is writing right away; he then uses the everyday model for everything. Resetting stops whatever the models are writing and unloads them, so they start clean (his memory stays).

Start over

Unlike the settings above, this changes Honk: it deletes his memory, journal, thoughts, the event archive and every program he wrote, and he wakes up new. It cannot be undone.
